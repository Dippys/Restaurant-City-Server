A community preservation search

Help us preserve Restaurant City

An old laptop, desktop, or hard drive may still hold pieces of the original Playfish game. We’re asking former players to help find those files before they disappear for good.

I may have an old computer How to help

Your old browser cache could be a time capsule

If you played Restaurant City on a computer that has been left mostly untouched, its browser cache may still contain original game assets.

The later 2011–2012 files are especially important.

2009→2012

How you can help

You don’t need to be technical. If you find a promising device—or know somebody who might have one—talk to us before changing anything on it.

Find an old device

Look for a computer or hard drive used to play Restaurant City between 2009 and June 2012. Ask family and friends, too.

Leave it untouched

Don’t clear browser history, run cleanup software, reinstall the browser, or reset the computer. Those actions can overwrite cached files.

Tell us on Discord

Share what device, operating system, and browser you remember using. We’ll help you check it carefully and answer questions.

⚠️

Protect your privacy: never upload an entire browser profile or hard drive. Old caches can contain unrelated personal information. Contact the team first so we can help isolate only the relevant game files.

What are we searching for?

Restaurant City downloaded Flash files and other assets while you played. Some of them may have survived locally even though the original servers are gone.

Files and addresses

A known game path used this versioned address:

static.playfish.com/game/cooking/swf/0.9.143a/

Useful SWF names may include:

gamegame_assetavatar_assetindoor_assetoutdoor_assetingredient_assetperk_assetsound_assetpreloaderpreloader_asset

Clues that you played a later version

The most useful caches may come from a version that included:

  • Day and night backgrounds based on local time
  • The option to open your restaurant for a set number of hours
  • Later decorations such as the bowling alley or airport lane

Earlier versions asked players to feed employees regularly. Those files are still welcome, but later versions are the biggest gap.

What we have

An early beta-era version has already been recovered from an old laptop. The search now focuses on finding a more complete, later build and preserving the original artwork and assets.

Have a device, a file, or just a lead?

Join the Restaurant City Reborn Discord. Even a vague memory, an old backup, a former developer contact, or a computer you’re unsure about may help move the search forward.
